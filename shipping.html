SHIPPING / DELIVERY POLICIES

We strive to process orders promptly. Delivery is made to the address you provide, so please ensure the delivery address is accurate. Start shopping now for your favourite sewing machines, parts, and accessories with ease on www.paysmallsmall.org.

NOTE: Delays may occur due to weather, public holidays, logistics challenges, or other unforeseen circumstances. While we work to ensure smooth delivery, delays may occasionally occur. If your order is significantly delayed or lost, please contact support for assistance.

If your item arrives damaged or incorrect, please report it within 24 hours of delivery and contact us with clear photo or video evidence to help us assist you quickly.

You will be informed of the estimated processing and delivery timeline.

The Delivery Time:
Delivery time varies depending on your location and the sewing machine, accessories, or parts purchased. Generally, estimated delivery within Lagos is 7–10 working days after dispatch.

This Shipping Policy explains how delivery works when you shop on www.paysmallsmall.org.

  • Delivery Time: Products are delivered within days after reaching the mid-point of your installment payment plan.
  • Processing Time: Orders typically have a processing time of 7–10 business days (excluding weekends and public holidays).
  • Delivery Timelines: Specific delivery timelines vary by location and product availability.
  • Service Area: Deliveries are currently restricted to Lagos and generally take 7 to 10 working days.

Signature Required:
A signature is mandatory upon delivery. Your signature is required to validate the credit sale agreement upon delivery. Kindly sign the attached contract confirming receipt and acceptance.

This is a standard practice to ensure both parties (PAYSMALLSMALL NIG. LTD and the Subscriber) are on the same page, preventing future disputes over whether an agreement was reached. We advise you to review the attached document thoroughly before providing your acknowledged signature.

The document provides your installment balance and the due date for each payment. PAY SMALL SMALL™ is a trusted equipment acquisition plan for fashion industry professionals.

Delivery Charges and Locations:
We do not offer free delivery; additional charges apply for remote addresses. Delivery fees are calculated at checkout based on the delivery address and are separate from the item price. The delivery cost depends on your location and the size of your sewing machine.

You will be informed of the delivery fee before your order is processed and shipped.

Customer Responsibilities and Failed Deliveries:
Customers should inspect products on delivery before signing. We shall not be responsible for damages or missing parts after acceptance. Unclaimed goods are returned to our depot, and a second delivery attempt will incur an additional fee.

For clarification or special delivery requests, please contact PAYSMALLSMALL NIG. LTD.
