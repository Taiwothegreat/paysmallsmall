Delete Your Pay Small Small Account

At Pay Small Small, we respect your privacy and your right to control your personal data.

To request deletion of your account and associated data:

  1. Send an email to: info@paysmallsmall.org
  2. Use the subject line: Account Deletion Request
  3. Include the email address linked to your account

Once verified, we will delete:

  • Your name
  • Email address
  • Phone number
  • Address
  • Account profile information
  • Wishlist and order history

Data that may be retained:

  • Transaction records required for legal, tax, or fraud-prevention purposes.

All deletion requests are processed within 7 business days.
