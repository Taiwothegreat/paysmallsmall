Frequently Asked Questions

1

What are the necessary conditions and requirements to participate in the installment plan?
    To qualify for our installment plan, you need to:
  • Sign up on our platform
  • Provide a valid ID card
  • Submit your BVN for auto debit
2

When will my sewing machine, parts, or accessories be delivered?

Delivery begins once you have paid at least fifty percent (50%) of the total cost through equal installments.

3

Can I discontinue my payment schedule after starting?

Yes, but only before delivery is processed. If you discontinue, you will receive a refund with a 10% deduction from your total installment paid.

4

What happens if I halt or default on my payment plan?

Late payments or defaults attract a penalty charge and may affect your eligibility for future plans. If the product has already been delivered, you may face forfeiture or additional fines as determined by management. In short: “Pay late, pay extra — or lose the item.”

5

Can I modify my order after making payment?

No. Once a payment plan is activated, product changes are not allowed. To switch to a different product, you must cancel the current plan and start a new one.

6

Can I buy multiple sewing machines or accessories in one transaction?

No. Each purchase must be completed one at a time. Finish one transaction before starting another.

What should I do if the product I want isn’t available on the Marketplace?

If a product isn’t listed on our Marketplace, please inform us immediately. We’ll analyze the details and add the relevant information or make the product available as soon as possible.

10

What happens if the price of the sewing machine reduces before delivery?

If the product’s price drops before your delivery, we will credit the difference (surplus) toward your subsequent installment payments.

11

What happens if the price of the sewing machine increases before delivery?

If the price increases, we will adjust the balance across your remaining installment payments. Note: Purchases depend on the availability of the item at the time of delivery.

12

Can I make a 50% payment to collect my sewing machine immediately?

Yes, partial payment of 50% is accepted for immediate collection, but only under a two-month or two-week plan, and it is subject to management verification . The final payment must be made as a single lump-sum transaction .

13

What are the terms for returning a product due to a change of mind?

If you decide to return a product simply because you’ve changed your mind, a 15% charge will apply to the total installment amount paid so far
